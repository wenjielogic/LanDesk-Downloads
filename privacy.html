隐私政策 · Privacy policy

LanDesk 局域桌面 · iPhone 应用及 Windows 配套电脑端

你的桌面，在你的设备之间传输

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日。开发者：Wenjie Lu。联系：luwenjie.nz@gmail.com。

LanDesk 通过局域网，把你批准共享的 Windows 主屏画面传给你的 iPhone，并在你允许时把鼠标、键盘操作传回电脑。应用没有用户账户、云中转、广告、行为分析或第三方追踪软件。开发者不接收或保存你的桌面画面、输入内容或配对凭据。

哪些信息会被使用

信息用途与保存方式
桌面画面与鼠标位置由电脑实时采集并发送到已配对手机，仅用于显示。应用不提供录制或保存截图功能；显示帧保存在内存，断开后清除。共享期间，主屏上的通知或私人内容也可能被手机看到。
鼠标、按键和输入文字只发往你批准的电脑，用于执行操作。未确认发送的文字草稿在本次 iPhone 应用进程内存中保留，断线时不自动重发，应用结束后不保留。你在电脑其他应用中输入或保存的内容受那些应用管理。
局域网地址、电脑名称、证书指纹与随机配对凭据用于选择、识别、核对和连接两台设备。配对二维码包含地址和一次性凭据。二维码最长 10 分钟有效，成功配对后失效。手机不会持久保存配对凭据、连接历史或桌面帧。
相机与手动粘贴相机仅在打开扫码页面时读取二维码，不保存相机照片或上传视频。拒绝相机权限仍可粘贴连接码。剪贴板内容只在你主动使用系统粘贴操作时读取，不做自动剪贴板同步。

连接保护与停止

两台设备之间使用 TLS 加密。手机按二维码中的证书指纹核对电脑，电脑端还须明确批准连接。Windows 的鼠标键盘控制默认关闭，需在开始共享前主动允许。凭据和会话只在内存中保存；主机生成的临时证书与私钥文件在载入后删除。手机进入后台、断开连接或电脑停止共享会结束会话并清除手机画面；网络突然断开时，电脑端的超时机制负责释放未松开的操作。不要向他人公开有效配对码。

开发者、服务商与支持邮件

应用本身不把使用数据发送给开发者或分析服务商。你主动打开支持/隐私网页或下载电脑端时，浏览器会连接 GitHub 托管服务，该服务可能按其政策处理 IP 地址、请求时间等访问记录；这不是桌面会话的云中转。详情见 GitHub 隐私声明。Apple 的应用分发和你选择共享的系统诊断受 Apple 的政策和设备设置管理。

如果你主动发邮件求助，开发者会收到邮箱地址、邮件内容及你附上的文件，并仅用于回复、排查和相关支持记录。邮件由邮件服务商处理。请不要提供不必要的个人资料、密码或有效配对码；可使用上述邮箱申请删除开发者控制范围内、不再需要且无保留义务的支持记录。

你的选择与政策变更

你可以停止共享、撤销控制权限，或在系统设置中撤销相机和局域网权限。应用没有需要删除的云账户。停止应用可结束内存中的会话和文字草稿；这不会删除你通过电脑其他应用保存的内容。若今后增加服务端、统计、账户或其他数据处理方式，我们会更新此政策和适用的应用隐私声明。

Your desktop stays between your devices

Effective 7 October 2026. Developer: Wenjie Lu. Contact: luwenjie.nz@gmail.com.

LanDesk sends the primary screen of a Windows PC you approve to your iPhone over your local network. When you permit control, it sends mouse, key and text commands back to that PC. There are no user accounts, cloud relays, advertising, behavioural analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. The developer does not receive or retain desktop frames, entered text or pairing credentials.

Information used to provide the connection

  • Desktop frames and pointer position: captured on the PC and sent to the paired phone for live display. The app does not offer recording or screenshot storage. Frames are held in memory and cleared on disconnect. Anything visible on the primary display, including notifications, may be visible to the paired phone.
  • Mouse, keys and entered text: sent only to the approved PC to perform your commands. Unconfirmed drafts remain in memory for the current iPhone app process, are not automatically resent after a disconnect, and are not persisted after termination. Content you enter or save in other Windows applications is managed by those applications.
  • Local addresses, PC name, certificate fingerprint and random credentials: used to identify and establish the connection. QR codes contain a local destination and one-time credential. Codes expire after up to 10 minutes and are consumed by successful pairing. The phone does not persist credentials, connection history or screen frames.
  • Camera and paste: camera access is used only while scanning a pairing QR. Camera photos and video are neither saved nor uploaded. Pasting the complete connection text is available without camera permission. Clipboard access occurs through your explicit system paste action; there is no automatic clipboard synchronisation.

Security and ending a session

Device-to-device transport uses TLS. The phone checks the certificate fingerprint from the QR and the PC must approve the pairing request. Control is off by default and must be enabled before sharing. Credentials and session state are memory-only. The host deletes temporary certificate and private-key files after loading them. Backgrounding or disconnecting the phone, or stopping the host, ends the session and clears the phone display. A host timeout releases held input if a network interruption prevents immediate cleanup. Do not share active pairing codes.

Developer access, hosting and support

The app sends no usage data to the developer or analytics partners. Opening a help or privacy page, or downloading the host, connects your browser to GitHub hosting. That provider may process request metadata such as IP address and time under its privacy statement. Website hosting is not part of desktop-session transport. Apple's distribution services and system diagnostics you choose to share are governed by Apple's policies and your device settings.

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address, message and attachments for responding, troubleshooting and related support records. Email is processed by the email provider. Do not include unnecessary personal information, passwords or live pairing codes. You may request deletion of support records within the developer's control that are no longer needed and are not subject to retention obligations.

Your controls and future changes

You can stop sharing, withdraw control permission, or revoke camera and local-network access in system settings. There is no cloud account to delete. Terminating the app ends memory-only sessions and drafts; it does not remove content you saved in other PC applications. If server features, accounts, analytics or other data processing are introduced, this policy and the applicable app privacy disclosures will be updated.
